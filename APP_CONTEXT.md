## What is Hub?

Hub is a comprehensive Customer Relationship Management (CRM) and enterprise management platform that combines sales pipeline management, lead tracking, contact management, project management, inventory control, and procurement workflows into a single unified platform.

Built for modern businesses that need a high-bandwidth command layer unifying payments, inventory, and logistics in a single synchronized system — purpose-built for teams that scale.

## What We Offer

### 🎯 **Sales Management Suite**

**Lead Management**
- Capture and qualify leads from multiple sources (website, referral, ads, imports)
- Intelligent lead scoring algorithm (0-100) based on completeness and engagement
- Automated status workflow: New → Contacted → Qualified → Unqualified → Converted
- One-click lead conversion to Contact + Deal
- CSV export and bulk operations

**Contact Management**
- Complete customer profiles with company, email, phone, and source tracking
- Relationship scoring (Cold/Warm/Hot) based on activity and recency
- Full activity timeline showing all interactions (calls, emails, meetings, notes)
- Link contacts to deals and opportunities for pipeline visibility

**Deal Pipeline**
- Visual sales pipeline with drag-and-drop stage management
- Deal health indicators (Green/Amber/Red) based on engagement recency
- Expected close date tracking and lost reason capture
- Multi-currency value tracking with automated reporting

**Quotes & Sales Orders**
- Auto-generated quote numbers with line items and pricing
- Stock reservation capabilities for quoted items
- Validity period tracking with auto-expiry and stock release
- Follow-up reminders at 50% of validity window
- Seamless conversion from quotes to sales orders
- Shipment tracking and stock deduction on delivery
